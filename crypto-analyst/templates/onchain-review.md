# Template: Onchain áttekintés

Cadence: monthly, or when flows regime-shift. Output: Hungarian.
Format: chart-dense, text-light (~1 chart per 2 sentences; 10–12 charts, ~3.5K chars).

## Structure

1. Salutation + purpose: "Körkép jelleggel megnézzük, milyen folyamatok zajlanak a
   blokkláncadatok szerint." + bullet list of *why now*.
2. Method note (verbatim spirit): "A kriptopiacot a BTC-n, mint általános trendszetteren
   keresztül vizsgáljuk, nem külön-külön altokra bontva."
3. **Metric walk** — each metric = colon-sentence → chart → 1–2 sentence interpretation.
   Standard set (rotate/extend as data warrants):
   - US spot BTC ETF net flows (regime + cumulative coins) and ETF holders' avg cost basis
     ("víz alatt" check) — note T+1 delay.
   - DAT/treasury offset flows (Strategy et al purchases vs ETF outflows).
   - Fear & Greed vs history.
   - Alt leverage / open interest cooling-heating.
   - Stablecoin supply (USDT/USDC deltas, chain split) — with HUF conversion
     ("forintosítva nagyjából … MRD HUF") — read as fiat redemption/inflow signal;
     tie to DXY + yields ("a kriptopiac kamat- és dollárerősség-érzékeny maradt").
   - MVRV Z-score vs prior cycle lows (calibrated: state the honest difference).
   - Whale cohorts (1,000+ BTC entities count vs holdings).
   - Supply in loss ("historikusan mindössze N alkalommal volt hasonló").
   - STH/LTH cost basis as support/resistance.
4. Close: **what to watch** (2–3 concrete tripwires: events + levels). No forecast.

## Interpretation grammar (every metric)
(a) what it *often* signals ("gyakran fiat visszaváltásokat jelöl") —
(b) historical analogue **with the honest difference** ("legalacsonyabb 2022 óta, de 0,27
vs. akkori negatív") — (c) cross-asset link (DXY, yields, gold/silver).

## Rules
- Free-tier data honesty: where a metric is a proxy for a paywalled one (Glassnode/
  CryptoQuant tier), label it ("közelítő adat"). Confidence tags required.
- Numbers on charts restated in text. Source tag per chart.
- Interpretations in potential mood (-hat/-het); divergences highlighted as the story.
