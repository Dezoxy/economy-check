# Analytical doctrine — the reasoning playbook

Distilled from the full KV corpus (132 posts, step B2 mining 2026-07-20), with
selection pressure applied from the graded ledger (N=50). Loaded by EVERY analyst
subagent and the composer, every report type. Cite a pattern **by name** when you
deploy it — the fact-checker audits reasoning provenance. Voice lives in
style-guide.md; formats in the templates; this file is reasoning only.

## A. Makró-értelmezés

1. **Motorháztető-elv** — never accept a headline number. Decompose: participation
   rate behind unemployment, import arithmetic behind GDP (imports subtract, so a
   strong dollar mechanically dents GDP), energy component behind CPI, one-off items
   behind earnings, sub-indexes (orders, prices) before the PMI headline. The
   reclassification (transient vs organic) IS the analysis. Track record: his
   strongest family — GDP-import ✅, GDPNow-statistical ✅ (07-19 vindicated), PCE-peak ✅.
2. **FedWatch-transzmisszió** — a print's real signal is the CME repricing it forces,
   stated before→after. When futures-implied path splits ~50/50 from the Fed's own
   path, volatility is pre-loaded: every print forces half the market to reposition.
3. **Nowcast-előny, print-alázattal** — real-time measures (GDPNow, truflation-type)
   front-run official data by weeks; the official print is often old news. BUT the
   05-03 miss is binding: nowcasts set directional expectation, never a print value.
4. **Soft-vs-hard hierarchia** — surveys are early snapshots, measured data decides;
   the divergence itself is a finding. Audit every survey's field window against
   events since (a pre-ceasefire-sampled survey is partially expired).
5. **Adat-évjárat arbitrázs** — when two series conflict, check sampling vintage;
   the fresher-sampled series wins. Pre-flagged one-offs (shutdown, strike) are
   already-priced, not news.
6. **Törött-indikátor szűrő** — before trusting a classic alarm (Hindenburg, Sahm,
   inverted curve), test whether its economic regime still exists (deficit-without-
   ceiling, BTFP, K-shaped economy break old base rates). 05-15 ✅: omen faded, ATHs followed.
7. **Második rendű lánc** — chase transmission two steps past the data point
   (home sales → rents → CPI; KRW → chip exports → device prices → CPI). Name every
   link with a number; if a link has no number, say so.

## B. Szintválasztás és TA

8. **Konfluencia-szabály** — a level earns attention ONLY where independent tools
   coincide: MA × forgalmi polc (volume shelf) × anchored VWAP × POC × structural
   boundary. Single-tool levels are noise. Track record: the 65,500 roadmap hit
   exactly (03-29); 200DMA rejection to the dollar (05-17).
9. **Rendezett fallback-útiterv** — every technical view ships as a roadmap: ONE
   upside proof (close above X), ONE veszélyzóna, and ordered supports below
   (Z1 → Z2 → Z3). Both directions, always — this is the family that made his
   calls gradeable and mostly right.
10. **Légzsák/FVG-elv** — low-volume zones under a support have no resting bids:
    losing the support means a fast gap-fill and mechanical liquidation cascade.
    Map FVGs BEFORE the break (06-02 ✅ "rendkívül gyors" fill as pre-mapped).
11. **RSI-struktúra mint kapu** — the RSI's own MA and trendlines are first-class
    objects; reclaiming them is necessary-but-not-sufficient. RSI≈70 = fragility:
    small catalysts cause outsized moves, no big cause needed.
12. **Mintázat-visszhang őszinte különbségekkel** — analogues allowed only with the
    disanalogy named in the same breath (what's different this time, with a number).
13. **Schelling-pont reflexivitás** — levels work because the crowd watches them;
    stops cluster just below recognized supports, so first breaks are often stop-runs.
    Validate levels with onchain realization spikes at the zone (D15).
14. **Rezsim-alapú TA-lefokozás** — in earnings-season, war-headline or K-divergence
    regimes, explicitly downgrade chart weight instead of forcing a read (04-24 ✅
    "ride fundamentals over TA" at ATH; 04-30 self-graded ✅).

## C. Pozicionáltság

15. **Spot-vs-futures dekompozíció** — before interpreting any sharp move, split it:
    OI/leverage-driven = fragile, spot-driven = durable. Resolve OI ambiguity with
    funding (rising OI + negative funding = shorts crowding). 04-19 ✅, 06-04 cascade ✅.
16. **Crowded-trade kontra-jelzés** — survey/positioning extremes (BofA FMS, funding,
    two-sided leveraged-product mania) are vulnerability maps, not direction calls:
    the crowded story doesn't have to be wrong, its holders are fragile. His best
    positioning family: silver-crowding → −50% ✅; semis 07-08 ✅ self-graded.
17. **Kényszer-flow vs önkéntes-flow** — attribute every selloff: liquidation share,
    cohort counts stable = leverage flush, not conviction exit. The attribution
    decides whether a dip is structural or mechanical.
18. **Strukturális vevő felülír** — identify price-insensitive flows (DAT programs,
    ETF creations, 401k schedules) with their corporate mechanics; they can override
    bearish TA signals (04-12 STRC ✅). Pair with **"nincs olyan, hogy soha"**: no
    absolutist behavior assumptions — check the filings for the flip condition
    (06-28: the pre-described STRC floor-break mechanism fired).
19. **Beárazottsági teszt** — after any scheduled event, verdict = outcome vs what
    derivatives already priced. "No new information" is a legitimate, publishable read.

## D. Onchain

20. **Kohorsz-létra torzítás-kontrollal** — read wallet tiers (1+/10+/100+/1k+/10k+)
    netted across tiers (redistribution ≠ exodus), ALWAYS with the custodian caveat:
    1000+ declines often mirror ETF custodian mechanics. Label proxies "közelítő adat".
21. **CEX-készlet két csatornája** — low exchange reserves matter via two named
    mechanisms only: leverage-spike demand outrunning supply, and ETF sourcing.
    If most supply sits ON exchanges (wrapped/blackbox), declare onchain unreadable.
22. **Realizálás-a-szinten** — profit/loss realization spiking exactly at pre-marked
    technical zones validates the level and diagnoses a technically-driven regime.
23. **STH/LTH cost basis** — STH basis reclaim = momentum switch (retest = confirm);
    LTH basis above spot inverts incentives. 05-10 ➖ warns: pair it with the 200DMA
    divide, never alone.
24. **Hipotézis-falszifikáció** — every flow narrative ships with a discriminating
    metric that could kill it (safe-haven claim → alt-ETF flows test). State the
    test, run it, accept the verdict.

## E. Geo / esemény

25. **Bázisráta-pánikfék** — war headlines → short volatility, not bear markets
    (S&P avg −4.6%, rebound ~6 weeks, ~60% of conflicts up in year 1) UNLESS the
    conflict converts into supply-shock → inflation → rate-path damage. That chain,
    not the headline, is the watch-point.
26. **Chokepoint-aritmetika** — quantify the actual flow share (Hormuz ~20%) AND the
    compensating factors (pipelines, SPR, reroutes) before any energy conclusion.
27. **Retorika-diszkont, fizikai lábnyom** — official statements are unweightable
    (negotiation posture); verify against non-fakeable data: tanker transits,
    deployments, auction calendars, the price itself. Actors' binding constraints
    (midterm calendar, refinancing costs, approval trackers) beat their rhetoric —
    but 07-05 oil ❌ warns: incentives lose to physical escalation; never a direction
    call here without an invalidation level.
28. **Forrás-hierarchia** — official channel > wire-cited named analyst with numbers
    > rumor; every claim carries its epistemic status label. Primary documents
    (13F/S-1/minutes) over commentary, with the lag/snapshot caveats said aloud.

## Diszciplína

- **Kivárás with ex-ante reasons** — waiting is a position: record the named reasons
  (his 3-reason 2025 kivárás → 02-06 confluence-triggered tranche entry ➖/vindicated).
- **Katasztrófa-súlyozott kockázat** — risk = probability × magnitude; with leverage
  the magnitude term dominates (flash-crash gaps) → no leverage, ever.
- **Exit-liquidity flip** — at saturation (>90% supply in profit, record leverage)
  the marginal buyer becomes someone's exit; flip to waiting and absorb being early.
- **FOMO-teszt** — a rally is chaseable only while its driver inventory (EPS
  revisions, scheduled flows, capex) outweighs its exhaustion inventory.
- **Anti-prophecy close** — scenario trees with levels, never forecasts;
  "Jósgömbje senkinek sincsen."
